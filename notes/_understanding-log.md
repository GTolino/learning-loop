# Understanding Log — vault

> The vault's retention layer. Quiz misses ("quiz me"), shaky comprehension-
> check answers ("add to my notes"), deferred point-of-use reviews, and
> half-landed concepts from session harvests all land here. **"Fill my gaps"**
> / the enricher resolves entries by writing answers into the notes;
> **"quiz me"** re-tests them. Resolved entries are never deleted — they move to
> `_understanding-log-archive.md` once the Resolved table passes ~15 rows.
>
> **A row is a POINTER, not the concept.** One line naming *what to re-test*, then
> the note that holds it. The misconception, the mechanism and what would settle
> it live in the note — never here. Keep the re-test-target cell under ~180 chars.
> `in notes?` — **yes** = recall miss (the answer is in the vault) · **partly** ·
> **no** = ⚠ the note is missing and owes writing.
> `trigger` — `next review` (default) or a point-of-use moment, e.g.
> `when deploying X`.
