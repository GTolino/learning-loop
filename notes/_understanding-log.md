# Understanding Log — vault

> The vault's retention layer. Quiz misses ("quiz me"), shaky comprehension-
> check answers ("add to my notes"), deferred point-of-use reviews, and
> half-landed concepts from session harvests all land here. **"Fill my gaps"**
> / the enricher resolves entries by writing answers into the notes;
> **"quiz me"** re-tests them. Never delete resolved entries — they are the
> record of what's been closed.
>
> `in notes?` — **yes** = recall miss (the answer is in the vault);
> **no** = true gap (an unknown unknown to add).
> `trigger` — when to resurface: `next review` (default) or a point-of-use
> moment, e.g. `when deploying X`.
